📚 Reading Passage

Beach or Park?
My favorite place is the beach. I like the beach because I love to swim. Also, at the beach I can build sandcastles with my sister. We laugh a lot. Some kids like the park more. They say the park is best because they can run and play with balls. Both the beach and the park are fun. But the beach will always be my favorite.

❓ Reading Comprehension

Answer each question in a complete sentence. Check the green box after you write your answer.

What is the writer's favorite place?Main IdeaThe beach.
Name two reasons the writer loves the beach.FactsHe can swim, and he can build sandcastles with his sister.
How is the park different from the beach in the story?CompareAt the park kids run and play with balls; at the beach the writer swims and builds sandcastles.
Is 'The beach will always be my favorite' a fact or opinion?Fact vs. OpinionAn opinion -- 'favorite' is what the writer thinks.

📐 Language & Grammar

Opinion Sentences with 'because'

An opinion tells what you think. A strong opinion sentence has a reason. Use because to give the reason.

Sentence starter: My favorite _____ is _____ because _____.
Example: My favorite food is rice because it is warm and yummy.

Try It:
  1. My favorite color is _____ because _____.
  2. My favorite subject is _____ because _____.
  3. My favorite friend is _____ because _____.
  4. My favorite place is _____ because _____.

✏️ Writing

Write your opinion: Which is better, the beach or the park? Give reasons!

Grade Differentiation

Grade 1

Draw your favorite place. Complete: 'My favorite is _____ because _____.'

Grade 2

Write a 3-sentence opinion: your favorite + 2 reasons.

Grade 3

Write a 5-sentence opinion paragraph: opinion + 3 reasons + closing sentence.
