📚 Reading Passage

The Sea Turtle
The green sea turtle is called honu in Hawaiian. Honu are very old animals -- some live longer than 80 years. Honu are big. A honu can weigh more than 300 pounds. Honu live in the warm ocean water around Hawaiʻi. They eat sea grass and algae. They come to the beach to rest. Honu are special. It is against the law to touch them. If you see a honu, stay 10 feet away.

❓ Reading Comprehension

Answer each question in a complete sentence. Check the green box after you write your answer.

What is the Hawaiian name for the green sea turtle?FactsHonu.
How long can a honu live?FactsLonger than 80 years.
What do honu eat?FactsSea grass and algae.
Why should we stay 10 feet away from a honu?ConclusionBecause it is against the law to touch them, and touching them can hurt them.

📐 Language & Grammar

Fact vs. Opinion

A fact is true and can be proved. → Honu can weigh 300 pounds.
An opinion is what someone thinks. → Honu are the cutest animal.

Look for words like best, cutest, worst, love, hate -- these often show opinions.

Try It:
  1. Sea turtles live in the ocean. (fact or opinion?)
  2. Honu are the best animals. (fact or opinion?)
  3. A honu can weigh 300 pounds. (fact or opinion?)
  4. Turtles are cute. (fact or opinion?)

✏️ Writing

Choose a favorite animal. Write and draw a mini-report.

Grade Differentiation

Grade 1

Draw the animal. Label 2 body parts. Write 1 fact.

Grade 2

Write a 3-fact mini-poster. Add a picture.

Grade 3

Write a 5-sentence report: name, where it lives, what it eats, one interesting fact, why you like it.
