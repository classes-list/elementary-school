📚 Academics

  • Communication is both written and oral. Students will understand and follow written and oral directions by reading and listening.
  • Explode the Code is used for phonics and reinforcement.
  • Spelling Skills cover spelling patterns, capitalization, and vocabulary development at the students’ level.
  • Language Arts covers parts of speech and sentence structure.
  • Reading Skills focus on Facts, Sequence, Context, Main Idea, Conclusion, and Inference.
  • Imagine Learning — Language & Literacy is an online reading program that focuses on vocabulary, comprehension, fluency, phonics, and phonemic awareness.
  • Writing Skills cover personal narratives, informative writing, descriptive writing, opinion and comparative writing, and poetry.

📊 Assessments

WIDA is a research-based organization that supports teachers of multilingual learners. It provides online assessments to help students develop academic language proficiency, focusing on advancing academic language development and achievement for multilingual students.

Imagine Learning provides online assessments that help teachers understand a student’s language and literacy needs. These assessments identify specific strengths and weaknesses, allowing for targeted instruction and intervention.

    (See handbook online for the full list.)

  • You should have received an ESL Supply List before school started. The required class supplies are: 1 wide-ruled composition book, 1 labeled 2-pocket portfolio, 1 Expo dry erase marker and eraser, 1 yellow highlighter, 12 pencils, 2 erasers, and 1 pack of 3×5 lined white index cards.
  • Technology — Your child will use their iPad in ESL class for online reading programs and assessments. Please be sure your child has their headphones in their homeroom class.
  • Homework is always due the next day. Please return the workbook to school. Homework should be a review of the day’s class work.
  • Daily class work and homework are checked to show thoroughness, effort, and neatness.
  • All tests are recorded in percentages.
  • Report Cards are given at the end of each quarter.
  • Parent Conferences are scheduled twice a year and as needed.
