Unit 1 · Q1 · Building the Foundation

All About Me

WIDA 1-2
Can-Do: I can introduce myself and name the people, colors, numbers, and feelings around me.

🎯 What You'll Learn

  • Say my name, age, and one favorite thing
  • Name colors and numbers 1-20
  • Draw and label pictures of my family
  • Read short-vowel words

📚 Reading Passage

Hi, I Am Sam
Hi! I am Sam. I am seven. I have a mom and a dad. I have a big sister and a small dog. My favorite color is red. My favorite food is rice. I feel happy at school. I like my friends.

📐 Language & Grammar

Naming Sentences (Subject + Verb)

A sentence tells one whole idea. It starts with a capital letter and ends with a period (.).

A simple sentence has a subject (who or what) and a verb (an action or being word).
Example: Sam runs.   I am happy.

Try It:
  1. _____ am seven.
  2. My mom _____ nice.
  3. The dog _____ small.
  4. _____ love my family.

✏️ Writing

Write and draw an "All About Me" page.

Grade Differentiation

Grade 1

Draw a big picture of yourself. Write your name and age.

Grade 2

Draw yourself. Write 2 sentences: your name, and one favorite thing.

Grade 3

Write a 3-sentence paragraph: name, age, and two favorite things. Add a picture.
