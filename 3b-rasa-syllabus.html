🌟 Our School Mission — Third Grade

Nurture

In 3rd Grade, nurture is an environment where students feel safe, supported, and valued — emotionally, socially, and academically.

Unite

In 3rd Grade, unite refers to creating a sense of togetherness, community, and belonging among students. It’s about building a classroom where everyone feels included, respected, and part of the group — like a family unit.

Inspire

In 3rd Grade, inspire means sparking students’ curiosity, creativity, and confidence — making them excited to learn and believe in their own abilities.

🌟 Classroom Rules & Expectations

Our class creates a classroom contract that establishes actions we take to make everyone feel like part of our community. Students participate in a collaborative discussion in which we agree upon the actions we want to see in the contract, and are expected to follow those agreed-upon expectations.

Other classroom expectations will be explicitly taught and demonstrated daily. Students are expected to work to high standards and are treated with warmth, respect, and fairness. I will contact you directly if your student is having any difficulties following classroom expectations that include:

  1. Be safe
  2. Be attentive
  3. Be respectful
  4. Be kind
  5. Do your best

📊 Grading

Daily class work and homework will be checked for timeliness, thoroughness, effort, and neatness. Writing assessments and class projects will be graded using rubrics. I will frequently be sitting down with students 1-on-1 to formally assess their academic progress. Throughout the school day I use informal, ongoing assessments to track student academic growth throughout the unit. I will then use summative assessments at the end of a unit or topic to see how much growth has been made and which areas still need improvement.

Report cards are given at the end of each quarter and are graded using a 1–4 scale.

1
Beginning
  • Student is beginning to understand the concept or skill
  • Requires significant support and guidance
  • Limited understanding/mastery of the topic
2
Developing
  • Student demonstrates partial understanding or skill
  • Needs occasional assistance or support
  • Progressing but with notable gaps
3
Proficient
  • Student meets grade level expectations
  • Demonstrates a solid understanding of concepts or skills
  • Works independently with minimal errors
4
Mastery
  • The student exceeds grade level expectations
  • Demonstrates a thorough understanding and can apply skills in new situations
  • Work is consistently accurate and shows higher level thinking

Report cards are based on how your child performs each quarter. Students also take a standardized assessment called STAR for early literacy and math in August and May. English Language Learners take the WIDA assessment as required.

📝 Homework Procedures

Students should do their best to complete the work on their own. However, they depend on support from both school and home for success. Encourage them to attempt the work independently before they ask for help.

  • Agenda Book: Each day your child will bring home their agenda book listing the day’s homework or any special notes/dates/reminders. Please initial the agenda book daily and return to school each day.
  • Homework Folder: Any homework worksheets will go home in a Homework Folder. Please keep paperwork on the left side of the folder titled Keep at Home and return homework on the right side of the folder titled Return to School. You will know I have checked the work if there is a star ⭐ or happy face 😊 at the top.

What is a Choice Board?

Generally homework will include a Choice Board (i.e., choose from a number of options) and a longer assignment like a Book Report that students will work on daily toward a final project. Homework will reflect what students have been learning in class and is expected to take about 40 minutes to complete each day.

📚 Curriculum Highlights

We employ the use of textbooks and a variety of supplementary iPad apps, as well as hands-on activities for our daily subjects. In addition, students receive training in PE, Music, Art, STEM, and Library.

✝️ Religion

RCL Benzinger is used as a guided resource. Growing in our faith is the core of our instruction. Learn to pray and follow the significant events in our liturgical calendar that teach us more about God.

📖 Literacy

Students will develop their reading comprehension, grammar/language, and writing skills through a cross-curricular approach that includes chapter books, online apps, and other resource materials. Students will explore fiction and non-fiction texts and write personal narratives, opinion pieces, research reports, poetry, and much more.

🧮 Math

Envision Math is a problem-based curriculum that will develop number sense, multiplication, division, time, data tables, fractions, and much more.

🔬 Science

Houghton Mifflin Harcourt, STEM Labs, and Mystery Science curriculum teaches students to explore engineering & technology, forces & motion, life cycles & inherited traits, organisms & their environment, and weather impact.

🌍 Social Studies

My World social studies curriculum teaches students about our environment, economics, communities, government, and citizenship. We will also learn about maps and geography.

💛 Health & Wellness

Health and wellness will be taught during Second Semester with our School Counselor, Ms. Tacy. Lessons are spread out over the entire semester, with two class periods devoted to each topic. Students explore self-awareness by recognizing and managing their emotions.
