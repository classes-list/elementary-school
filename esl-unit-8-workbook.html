📐 Language & Grammar

Rhyme + Sensory Words

Rhyming words end with the same sound. → cat, hat, bat, sat.

Sensory words paint a picture using the five senses.
• see: bright, shiny, huge, tiny
• hear: loud, quiet, splash, boom
• feel: soft, cold, smooth, warm
• taste: sweet, salty, sour
• smell: fresh, fishy, sweet, smoky

Try It:
  1. Find one word that rhymes with 'day.'
  2. Find one word that rhymes with 'star.'
  3. Give one word that describes something you can HEAR.
  4. Give one word that describes something you can FEEL.

Write your own short poem.

Grade Differentiation

Grade 1

Grade 2

Write an acrostic poem using your name. One word per letter.

Grade 3

Write a 4-line poem about your favorite place. Include one sensory word from each sense you can.
