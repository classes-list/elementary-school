💜 Guiding Philosophy

Kindergarten is a year of enormous growth. Five- and six-year-olds thrive in a warm, structured environment where they feel safe to be curious, safe to try, and safe to make mistakes. Learning at this age happens best through play, song, movement, story, and hands-on exploration. Every day in K1 blends structured academic work with joyful discovery, so children build strong foundations in reading, writing, and math while also learning how to be kind classmates, patient friends, and prayerful children of God.

At Mary, Star of the Sea School, we guide our students to become S.T.A.R.s:

  • ⭐ Self-aware, Confident Individuals
  • ⭐ Teachable, Lifelong Independent Learners
  • ⭐ Active Christians
  • ⭐ Responsible Citizens

🌟 Class Expectations

In K1 our simple rules guide us every day:

  • Be Kind — use kind words, kind hands, and kind hearts.

  • Do Your Best — try hard, keep trying, and be proud of your effort.

📊 Grading

I’ll be checking daily classwork and homework for effort, thoroughness, and neatness. Writing assessments and class projects will be graded using rubrics. I also spend time one-on-one with students to formally assess their academic progress. Throughout the day I use informal assessments to monitor progress during a unit. At the end of a unit or topic, I’ll conduct a summative assessment to evaluate overall progress and identify areas that may still require improvement.

Report cards go out at the end of each quarter and are graded on a 1–4 scale. Report cards are based on how your child performs each quarter — grades are not reflected based on year-end benchmarks.

1
Beginning
  • The student is beginning to understand the concept or skill
  • Requires significant support and guidance
  • Limited understanding or mastery of the topic
2
Developing
  • The student demonstrates partial understanding or skill
  • Needs occasional assistance or support
  • Progressing but with notable gaps
3
Proficient
  • The student meets grade-level expectations
  • Demonstrates a solid understanding of concepts or skills
  • Works independently with minimal errors
4
Mastery
  • The student exceeds grade level expectations
  • Demonstrates a thorough understanding and can apply skills in new situations
  • Work is consistently accurate and shows a higher level of thinking

Kindergartners also take the STAR Early Literacy assessment in fall, winter, and spring to track growth in phonics and reading. English Language Learners take the WIDA assessment as required.

Parent-Teacher Conferences are held after Q1 and Q3.

📝 Homework Routine & B.E.E. Binder (Bring Everything Everyday) 🐝

The B.E.E. Binder is your child’s daily tool for organization and communication between home and school. It must be brought to school every day. Homework begins August 10 and is completed Monday–Thursday nights (none on Fridays or during long breaks).

Daily Homework

  • Kindergarten Sight Words. At the beginning of the year, we spend one week mastering each sight word. As students gain confidence, we gradually increase the pace to one word every three days, every two days, or one word per day, depending on the word’s difficulty and the class’s readiness.
  • Please follow the Sight Word Calendar. Practice only the assigned word until all six words in that list have been mastered. The calendar will indicate when to review and practice the entire list for fluency. Please avoid introducing future sight words early — the sequence is designed to support long-term mastery.
  • Homework Packet. The weekly homework packet is located in the two-pocket folder inside your child’s BEE Binder. Sent home every Monday and due Friday (or the last school day before a long break). Packet includes:
    • Cover sheet with weekly verbal practice and handwriting page suggestions
    • Math pages
    • Phonics / Reading pages
  • Flashcards as supplemental practice for letter-sound correspondence, upper- and lowercase letter identification, sight words, and number recognition.

📚 Reading Folders

Students will receive reading folders with books at their reading level starting in September. Reading folders go home on Monday with books for the week. Books are to be returned in the reading folder at the end of the school week in order to get a new set of books on Monday.

🎂 Birthday Protocols 🎈

Celebrating birthdays in K1

  • We will sing Happy Birthday, and your child will receive a birthday crown on or near their special day.
  • Please notify me at least 1 week in advance if you plan to send in a treat.

    • Delivered to the front office (not directly to the classroom)
