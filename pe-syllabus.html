Physical Education Syllabus

Primary Physical Education (2nd-3rd Grade) · SY 2026-2027
Instructor: Mr. Lyman Lacro · Email: llacro@starofthesea.org
Physical Education (PE) is an important component in our schools’ curriculum, from primary to middle school. The overall objective of the subject is to equip our students with the knowledge, skills, and attitude to lead a physically active and healthy lifestyle throughout their life.

👋 Grade-Level Focus

In grades 2–3 Physical Education, students develop fundamental motor skills, coordination, and balance through a variety of fun and engaging activities. Lessons focus on teamwork, sportsmanship, and following rules while participating in individual and group games.

Students learn the basics of fitness — including stretching, endurance, and strength-building exercises appropriate for their age. Movement activities integrate skills such as throwing, catching, jumping, and running. The course fosters a lifelong appreciation for active living and healthy habits.

Students will also experience the fundamental skills needed to play various sports including volleyball, basketball, baseball, and football.

Students will take a fitness test at the beginning and the end of the school year.

📅 Class Schedule & Expectations

Students will have P.E. classes twice a week. They are expected to:

  • Give their best effort while participating in all activities
  • Display respect and sportsmanship toward fellow classmates
  • Learn rules and regulations for all activities
  • Be properly dressed in the P.E. uniform

👕 Uniform

Students may come to school dressed in their P.E. uniform on P.E. days.

📊 Grading Policy

Grading is based on active participation and skill development. A separate conduct grade is given for behavior.

🩹 Attendance

Students must bring in a doctor or parent note to excuse them from P.E. classes.
