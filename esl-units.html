School Year 2026-2027

🌐 ESL Unit Calendar · Grades 1–3

Mrs. Tanaka · Mary, Star of the Sea School

Eight thematic units across four quarters, aligned to the WIDA English Language Development Standards and the ESL syllabus. Each unit builds all four language domains — Listening, Speaking, Reading, and Writing — while integrating Explode the Code phonics, Imagine Learning, and grade-level ELA skills.

📖 How to read a unit card

Each unit lists the WIDA proficiency level (1 Entering → 4 Expanding), the Can-Do statement students work toward, the phonics book, the reading strategy from the syllabus (Facts, Sequence, Context, Main Idea, Conclusion, Inference), the writing focus, and how the same theme is differentiated for Grades 1, 2, and 3.

🌐 WIDA at a Glance — Grades 1–3

The WIDA English Language Development Standards describe how multilingual learners use language to succeed in school. Every unit above builds all four language domains and moves students along the WIDA proficiency continuum. Progress is measured yearly by the ACCESS for ELLs assessment.

The four language domains: Listening · Speaking · Reading · Writing

Level 1 · EnteringSingle words, pictures, gestures. Yes/no responses.
Level 2 · EmergingShort phrases and simple sentences with visual support.
Level 3 · DevelopingExpanded sentences; short paragraphs on familiar topics.
Level 4 · ExpandingConnected discourse; grade-level texts with some support.
Level 5 · BridgingGrade-level language with minimal support.
Level 6 · ReachingFull grade-level proficiency in academic English.
